Files in this folder are Kenney's Furniture Kit GLBs, resolved per furniture
item name via lib/furnitureKit.ts (keyword match) with a category-level
fallback (CATEGORY_DEFAULTS) and legacy aliases (Decor -> Plant,
Other -> Electronics). Filenames match the kit's original GLTF names.
